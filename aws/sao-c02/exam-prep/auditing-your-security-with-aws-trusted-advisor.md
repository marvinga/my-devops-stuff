### Auditing Your Security with AWS Trusted Advisor

- This lab guides you through the steps to audit your AWS resources to ensure your configuration complies with basic security best practices.
- This lab makes use of AWS Trusted Advisor as it applies to security.
- The topics covered include working with security groups, multifactor authentication (MFA), and AWS Identity and Access Management (IAM).

Congratulations! You now have successfully:

Used AWS Trusted Advisor to perform a basic audit of your AWS resources
Modified Amazon EC2 Security Groups to meet best practices
Configured Multi-factor Authentication (MFA)
